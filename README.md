
# Bright Motors Car Sales Analysis

**Project Overview**

This project was completed as part of the BrightLearn Data Analytics Case Study: Bright Motors Car Sales Analysis.

The objective was to analyze historical vehicle sales data and provide actionable insights to support the newly appointed Head of Sales in improving dealership performance, expanding the dealership network, optimizing inventory management, and increasing profitability.

The project combines data processing, SQL analysis, data visualization, and executive reporting to transform raw sales data into meaningful business recommendations.

**Business Problem**

Bright Motors required a data-driven understanding of:

-Which vehicle makes and models generate the highest revenue
-How selling price relates to mileage and vehicle age
-Which regions produce the highest sales volumes
-Customer purchasing trends and preferences
-Opportunities to improve profitability and operational 

**Project Objectives**

The main goals of the analysis were to:

1.Identify top-performing vehicle brands and models
2.Analyze revenue and sales trends
3.Evaluate regional performance
4.Examine vehicle pricing patterns
5.Calculate profitability metrics
6.Develop strategic recommendations for executive decision-making
